\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\mbox{\hyperlink{class_kicks}{Kicks}} similar to Broekgaarden+21 (\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.02608.pdf}{\texttt{ https\+://arxiv.\+org/pdf/2103.\+02608.\+pdf}}) The ECSN and Ultra stripped SN receives a kick from a Maxwellian with sigma= 15 km/s Ultra stripped SN are naked\+CO or star stripped by the HE layers while they are pur\+He stars (case BB), we flag a SN as Ultra stripped if it is a naked\+CO or a naked\+He with MHE-\/\+MCO\texorpdfstring{$<$}{<}0.\+1 (see Broekgaarden+21) the CCSN from a Maxwellian with sigma set by the parameter sn\+\_\+kick\+\_\+velocity\+\_\+stdev. The BH kick is NOT corrected for the fallback fraction.
